Daily Practices
🌅
Morning Breath
Take 5 slow deep breaths each morning. Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 2, out for 6. Reduces cortisol and helps baby too.
🚶‍♀️
Gentle Walking
A 20-minute walk each day — even in your compound — reduces anxiety, improves sleep, and eases back pain.
🗣️
Talk to Someone
Share your worries with your partner, mother, or a trusted friend. You are not meant to carry this journey alone.
🎵
Sing to Baby
Your baby can hear from week 18. Singing familiar songs or playing gentle music builds early bonding and calms both of you.
